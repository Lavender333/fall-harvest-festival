Explore the movement
Her story

A lifelong connection.
A growing purpose.

Victoria’s path began with her family’s dry-cleaning business in Glenville. Exploring environmentally safer practices helped inspire a new direction: growing food and creating community green space.

Through Green Movement Glenville, she has connected young people and families with practical growing skills and opportunities to learn together.

Biography informed by Ohio Aerospace Institute’s published profile of Victoria Trotter .

Coming soon · Introducing our future clothing line

Sustainable vision.
Beautiful, everyday style.

Ms. Victoria’s vision is growing in a beautiful new direction: a future clothing line with sustainability at its heart. The goal is to bring together stylish design, lasting beauty, and a more thoughtful approach to fashion.

Envisioned for people who want to express themselves with confidence, the line will celebrate individuality and the belief that caring for the earth can be part of how we dress. Beautiful to wear. Thoughtful in purpose. Full of possibility.

Concept illustrations exploring the future line. Final designs and materials are still to come.

Something special is taking shape. Collection details and a launch date will be shared as the vision develops.
