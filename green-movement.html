Rooted in Glenville

A place to grow.

Founded by Victoria Trotter, Green Movement Glenville brings urban agriculture, youth learning, and community connection together.

Grow

Fresh food

The farm grows fruit, vegetables, and herbs, helping neighbors connect with food close to home.

Learn

Young people, new possibilities

Hands-on agricultural learning builds teamwork, responsibility, and entrepreneurial skills.

Connect

Community green space

Turning vacant spaces into growing spaces creates room for neighbors to come together.
